# Chapters 2–5 monster redesign

Four transparent source sheets generated with built-in image_gen: six character rows per chapter (shield, drone, bomb, ghost, rubble, boss), four pose columns. Total 24 designs. Row cut metadata follows inspected content rather than assuming equal rows. Runtime atlas connects skins to existing actor states and keeps existing render heights and collision bodies. Chapter 1 unchanged. Originals retained; v2 files are separate. Prompts recorded in prompts.json.

Chapter 2: dollar banknotes, FX coins, institutional hands. Chapter 3: famous technology logo-inspired creatures. Chapter 4: bank facade, printing machine, elderly investor specter and central-bank brawler. Chapter 5: bull/bear, bubble, conglomerate and market emperor. These are stylized motifs, not endorsements.
